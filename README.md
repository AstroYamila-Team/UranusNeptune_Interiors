# Uranus Neptune Interiors

This repository contains the data presented in our paper: 'Reassessing planetary composition: Evidence of rock-dominated envelopes in Uranus and Neptune', Vanesa Ramirez, Yamila Miguel, Saburo Howard, 2026.

The files with the data produced in this work and used to make the plots are:

Neptune_fiducial.csv: Interior models calculated for Neptune fiducial ice+rock model, adopting REOS for water and the SESAME Dry Sand EOS for the rocky component.

Neptune_water_ses.csv: Interior models calculated for Neptune ice+rock model, adopting the SESAME water EOS and the SESAME Dry Sand EOS for the rocky component.

Neptune_rock_qeos.csv: Interior models calculated for Neptune ice+rock model, adopting REOS for water and QEOS for SiO2 for the rocky component.

Neptune_iceonly.csv: Interior models calculated for Neptune ice-only model, adopting REOS for water.

Uranus_fiducial.csv: Interior models calculated for Uranus fiducial ice+rock model, adopting REOS for water and the SESAME Dry Sand EOS for the rocky component.

Uranus_water_ses.csv: Interior models calculated for Uranus ice+rock model, adopting the SESAME water EOS and the SESAME Dry Sand EOS for the rocky component.

Uranus_rock_qeos.csv: Interior models calculated for Uranus ice+rock model, adopting REOS for water and QEOS for SiO2 for the rocky component.

Uranus_iceonly.csv: Interior models calculated for Uranus ice-only model, adopting REOS for water.

COLUMN UNITS

mcore: Earth mass

p12: Mbar

T0: K

req: Km
